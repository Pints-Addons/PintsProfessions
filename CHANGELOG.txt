Pint's Professions - Changelog

v1.0.3
- Levelling guide: right-click a step to leave that recipe out (the guide works it out again without it); Put back
  returns them, and its tooltip lists what's left out.
- Cooldown recipes (Mooncloth, transmutes) are left out of the levelling guide - untick Leave out cooldown recipes to
  use them. On WoW Forever it also leaves out the Cenarion Circle patterns (Swarmtender's, Venomspew), which need a
  Loom only a Tailoring 300 can make.
- WoW Forever: the levelling guide checks itself against wow-professions.com's Forever guides - a star on steps they
  pick too, and each step's tooltip says what they'd make there and where to learn the recipe (WowProfGuide.lua).
- Levelling guide: Auction recipes (on by default) also uses drop and quest recipes that are for sale on the auction
  house, counting what the recipe went for at your last full scan. The step shows (AH 12g) - its tooltip has the full price - and
  Build shopping list adds it.

v1.0.2
- Item tooltips show what the item costs to craft - each reagent at the cheapest of your latest Pint's Auctions full
  scan, a vendor, or making it yourself - with how old the scan is. Recipes show what the item they teach costs to
  make. Hold Shift for the reagents. /pprof tooltip turns it off (or on).

v1.0.1
- WoW Forever: Cooking, First Aid, Enchanting and Mining plan with Forever's own recipes too - skill colours, reagents,
  how each is learned and what it costs (from Wowhead's WoW Forever database), like Alchemy, Blacksmithing,
  Engineering, Leatherworking and Tailoring already did.
- WoW Forever: reagent vendor prices are Forever's (vials, water and spices cost more there).

v1.0
- First release: the cheapest way to level a profession at your auction house's prices (from Pint's Auctions
  full scans), usable anywhere; extra for bad luck on yellow and green recipes; use up the mats in your bags
  and bank first; Flip for profit (vendor and auction house); buy from the planner at the auction house.
  Moved out of Pint's Auctions - your settings come with it.
- Flip for profit: "Craft and sell (buy the mats)" lists recipes whose mats cost less than what the result sells
  for at a vendor or the auction house (Sell crafts: Vendor / Auction house).
- WoW Forever Tailoring: levelling guide and Flip use Forever's own recipes (skill colours, reagents, trainer and vendor
  costs) from Wowhead's WoW Forever database; Classic/Season of Discovery tailoring recipes Forever lacks aren't suggested.
- A "Pint's Professions" button under every profession window, beside Recipe Sources' Learnable: opens the levelling
  guide for that profession (right-click: Flip for profit). It replaces the small Level guide button at the top.
- Levelling guide: Tab moves between the From skill and to boxes, selecting what's there so you can type over it.
- Levelling guide: "Build shopping list" puts what the plan needs from the auction house into a Pint's Auctions shopping
  list ("Level Tailoring 177-200"), opened straight away at the auction house.
- WoW Forever Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Engineering and Leatherworking: the levelling guide and Flip use Forever's own
  recipes for these too (skill colours, reagents, trainer and vendor costs).
