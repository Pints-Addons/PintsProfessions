Pint's Professions v1.0 - the cheapest way to level a profession, and what to craft for a profit
(WoW Classic Era / WoW Forever)

WORKS WITH
  Pint's Auctions (recommended): do a full scan at the auction house and every price is saved, so the
  guide works anywhere afterwards and is worked out again after each new full scan. Without it, only
  vendor prices are known.
  Pint's Alts (optional): your bank's contents when you haven't opened it this session.

HOW TO USE
  /pprof, the minimap button, "Level guide" in your profession window, or "Professions" beside the
  auction house.
  Cheapest levelling
  - Pick a profession and the skill to start from (any number, or Mine for your own) and to reach.
  - Each step: what to craft, how many times (yellow and green recipes miss sometimes - extra for bad
    luck is built in: average, 85% or 95% sure), the cost and the cost per point. Training ranks
    (Journeyman, Expert, Artisan) are shown where you'll need them.
  - Reagents are priced the cheapest way: auction house (the market price of your last full scan),
    vendor, or made yourself (bolts from cloth, bars from ore).
  - "Use what's in my bags & bank first": reagents you have count at what a vendor would pay for them,
    so the guide uses them up and frees bag space. The shopping list shows what's yours and what to buy.
  - Optional: count what you get back selling what you make (vendor - sure; or auction - if the market
    holds).
  Flip for profit
  - Recipes whose reagents cost less than what you make sells for - to a vendor (sure) or on the
    auction house (if the market holds). Both have their own column and tick box.
  - Buy and vendor: items listed for less than a vendor pays.
  - At the auction house: pick one and "Buy selected" opens it (or each reagent) in the Buy view.
  Skill-up colours are learned from your profession windows (WoW Forever changed some), and the real
  skill-up rate of your own yellow/green crafts is logged and blended in (/pprof skillups).

COMMANDS
  /pprof            levelling guide        /pprof flip      Flip for profit
  /pprof skillups   your skill-up log      /pprof minimap   hide or show the minimap button
